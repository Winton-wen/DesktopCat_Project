## Character Lock

# DesktopCat Character Lock

Every accepted asset must depict the same kitten:

- 3D Q-style cream orange-and-white kitten.
- Big head, short round plush body, front-facing cute proportions.
- Cream orange tabby markings on forehead, cheeks, body, and tail.
- White muzzle, chest, belly, and paws.
- Large round deep brown glossy eyes with clear highlights.
- Small pink nose, tiny soft `w` mouth, pale pink blush.
- Big triangular ears with pink-orange inner ears.
- Short fluffy curled tail with orange rings.
- Large pink-brown checked bow centered at the neck.
- Small shiny gold bell centered on the bow.
- Clean transparent background, no scenery, text, watermark, or guide marks.

Rejected immediately:

- Missing bow or bell.
- Changed eye color, species, body proportions, or render style.
- Cropped ears, paws, tail, bow, or bell.
- Background-removal damage on white fur, whiskers, bow texture, or bell.
- Any frame that looks like a different kitten from the stable baseline.

## Character Identity

One consistent QQ-pet-style desktop companion: a soft premium 3D cartoon cream
orange-and-white kitten, plush toy proportions, big head, short round body,
large glossy deep brown eyes, small pink nose, pale blush, cream-orange tabby
stripes, white muzzle/chest/belly/paws, fluffy curled ringed tail, large
pink-brown checked bow at the neck, shiny gold bell centered on the bow,
transparent background, clean silhouette, warm soft lighting.

`blink`: same idle pose, eyes gently closing and reopening, closed-eye hold,

## Output Contract

- Frame Count: 10
- FPS Target: 10
- Canvas: 512x512 RGBA transparent PNG
- Full body visible unless the action intentionally curls or sleeps
- Keep bow and bell visible and consistent
- First and last frames must be idle-compatible for non-idle actions
- No guide marks, background, text, watermark, or cutout damage

## Negative Prompt

No realistic cat, no different species, no missing bow, no missing bell, no
wrong eye color, no cropped ears, no cropped tail, no damaged white fur, no
watermark, no text, no background, no scenery, no guide marks, no sticker
outline, no inconsistent lighting, no different character.
