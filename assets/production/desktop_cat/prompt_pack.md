# DesktopCat Prompt Pack

Use these as source prompts for pose sheets or single action frames. Keep the
character lock text intact between generations.

## Character Identity

One consistent QQ-pet-style desktop companion: a soft premium 3D cartoon cream
orange-and-white kitten, plush toy proportions, big head, short round body,
large glossy deep brown eyes, small pink nose, pale blush, cream-orange tabby
stripes, white muzzle/chest/belly/paws, fluffy curled ringed tail, large
pink-brown checked bow at the neck, shiny gold bell centered on the bow,
transparent background, clean silhouette, warm soft lighting.

## Negative Prompt

No realistic cat, no different species, no missing bow, no missing bell, no
wrong eye color, no cropped ears, no cropped tail, no damaged white fur, no
watermark, no text, no background, no scenery, no guide marks, no sticker
outline, no inconsistent lighting, no different character.

## Action Prompts

`idle`: front sitting pose, paws together, full body visible, tail curled to
the side, calm cute expression, idle-compatible baseline.

`blink`: same idle pose, eyes gently closing and reopening, closed-eye hold,
no body jump.

`wave`: same kitten, sitting pose, one front paw lifted beside face and waving,
starts and ends close to idle, bow and bell remain centered.

`clicked`: surprised cute pop reaction, ears perk, front paws raised slightly,
open small mouth, then settle back to idle-compatible pose.

`happy`: smooth joyful reaction with animation in-betweens: starts from idle,
small anticipation dip, eyes brighten gradually, one front paw lifts through
several intermediate poses, soft head/body bounce with slight overshoot, tail
perks with delayed secondary motion, then settles back to idle. No one-frame
paw pop, no duplicate paw, no sudden pose swap.

`sleep_in`: natural sleepy settle-down sequence: starts from idle, slow blink,
head lowers first, shoulders relax, front paws tuck or slide softly, body leans
into a curled sleeping pose while keeping volume, tail follows with delayed
motion, ends matching the sleep loop. Do not flatten, melt, squash, or collapse
the whole cat.

`sleep`: curled sleeping pose, eyes closed, tail near body, subtle breathing,
bow and bell still visible.

`wake`: natural wake-up transition from sleep to idle: eyes open first, head
lifts gradually, front paws push the body up, ears perk after the head rises,
tail gives a delayed small flick, ends close to idle. No instant one-frame sit
up, no hard jump.

`walk`: right-facing cute walking cycle, readable alternating front and back
paws, subtle body bob, bow and bell lag slightly, tail follows with secondary
motion, seamless loop-capable cycle, full body visible.

`walk_left`: left-facing cute walking cycle matching walk quality, readable
alternating paws, subtle body bob, bow and bell lag slightly, tail follows with
secondary motion, seamless loop-capable cycle, full body visible.

`cute`: high-fps cute "acting adorable" reaction: starts from idle, tiny
anticipation bounce, soft blink or sparkling eyes, head/body tilts gently side
to side, one paw or both paws tuck near the bow, cheeks look extra soft, tail
flicks lightly, then returns to idle. No harsh pose jump, no extra limb.

`drag`: front paws slightly lifted, soft hanging body, puzzled cute expression,
slow movement, no fast head shake.
