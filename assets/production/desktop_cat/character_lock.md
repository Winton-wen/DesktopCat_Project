# DesktopCat Character Lock

Every accepted asset must depict the same kitten:

- 3D Q-style cream orange-and-white kitten.
- Big head, short round plush body, front-facing cute proportions.
- Cream orange tabby markings on forehead, cheeks, body, and tail.
- White muzzle, chest, belly, and paws.
- Large round deep brown glossy eyes with clear highlights.
- Small pink nose, tiny soft `w` mouth, pale pink blush.
- Big triangular ears with pink-orange inner ears.
- Short fluffy curled tail with orange rings.
- Large pink-brown checked bow centered at the neck.
- Small shiny gold bell centered on the bow.
- Clean transparent background, no scenery, text, watermark, or guide marks.

Rejected immediately:

- Missing bow or bell.
- Changed eye color, species, body proportions, or render style.
- Cropped ears, paws, tail, bow, or bell.
- Background-removal damage on white fur, whiskers, bow texture, or bell.
- Any frame that looks like a different kitten from the stable baseline.
