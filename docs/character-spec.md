# DesktopCat Character Bible

## Goal

Create one consistent QQ-pet-style desktop companion. V1 focuses on polished animation, mouse click interaction, dragging, and speech bubbles. Do not add養成 systems yet.

## Canonical Character

The pet is one 3D Q-style cream orange-and-white kitten based on the images in `参考图`.

Must always keep:

- Big head, short round body, plush toy proportions.
- Cream orange fur with white muzzle, chest, belly, and paws.
- Soft orange tabby stripes on forehead, cheeks, body, and tail.
- Large round deep brown glossy eyes with clear white highlights.
- Small pink nose, tiny soft `w` mouth, pale pink blush.
- Big triangular ears with soft pink-orange inner ears.
- Thin pale whiskers on both sides.
- Short fluffy curled tail with orange ring markings.
- Large pink-brown checked bow at the neck.
- Small shiny gold bell centered on the bow.
- Soft premium 3D cartoon render style, clean silhouette, warm edge light.

Must never:

- Change species or make it realistic.
- Remove, recolor, shrink too much, or move the bow and bell inconsistently.
- Change eye color away from deep brown/glossy black-brown.
- Damage white fur, whiskers, bow texture, or bell during transparency extraction.
- Use messy sticker cutouts, warped screenshots, text, watermark, scenery, or complex background.

## V1 Required Actions

- `idle`: front sitting, paws together, subtle breathing, occasional blink, slow tail sway.
- `blink`: same pose, clean eye close/open motion.
- `clicked`: surprised cute reaction, ears perk, body pops slightly.
- `happy`: small bounce, bright eyes, stronger blush, tail perk.
- `wave`: sitting pose, one front paw raised and waving.
- `sleep`: curled or lying down, eyes closed, tail near body, subtle breathing.
- `walk`: short-leg alternating walk, stable scale and baseline.
- `drag`: front paws slightly lifted, body soft/hanging, puzzled but cute expression.

## V1 Deferred

Eating, stretching, anger, status panels, feeding mechanics, shops, mini-games, and complex settings are deferred until the core animation quality is accepted.
